# Solo Attorney Admin & Drafting Kit

**Reclaim billable hours from client-comms and first-draft overhead.**
A done-for-you system for solo attorneys and paralegals: 12 prompts for the
non-advice administrative and first-draft work — client intake, engagement
letters, status updates, plain-English explainers, and internal drafting starts
— every one gated behind mandatory attorney review.

- **Tier:** Core — **$89**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion tracker
  (`workflow.md`) + disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** Solo & small-firm attorneys, paralegals, legal ops
- **License:** Personal use (single attorney/paralegal). Firm license available.

## Why this exists

The average US lawyer bills ~$349/hr (corporate ~$461, DC ~$492). Every hour lost
to intake emails, status updates, and blank-page drafting is expensive. This kit
compresses the **administrative and first-draft** layer — explicitly **not**
legal advice or work product that leaves the office without attorney review.

## The ROI

At $349/hr, two hours a week reclaimed is ~$35,000/yr of billable capacity. Even
after the mandatory attorney-review step, the kit removes the blank page from the
repetitive, low-complexity communications that clog a solo practice.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six tasks:
   - Client intake & screening emails
   - Engagement / retainer letter drafts
   - Client status-update emails
   - Demand-letter first drafts (internal)
   - Discovery / document summaries (from provided material)
   - Plain-English client explainers
2. **`workflow.md`** — an intake-to-matter pipeline and a Notion matter tracker,
   with the attorney-review checkpoint everywhere.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — the unauthorized-practice-of-law (UPL) overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the task, copy the prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]`.
2. **Treat every output as an internal first draft.** Nothing goes to a client,
   opposing counsel, or a court without licensed-attorney review (see
   `DISCLAIMER.md`).
3. **Never rely on the model for legal conclusions, citations, or jurisdiction-
   specific rules** — verify everything.
4. Track the matter and the review step in the Notion tracker.

## The compliance edge (severe)

Legal carries the heaviest overlay of any niche: unauthorized-practice-of-law
risk, jurisdiction issues, confidentiality, and AI-hallucinated citations that
have gotten real lawyers sanctioned. This kit positions strictly as admin +
first-draft support with **"attorney review required" on every template** — the
guardrail casual prompt-packs don't provide.
