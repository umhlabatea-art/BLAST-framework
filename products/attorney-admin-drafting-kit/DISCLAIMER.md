# Disclaimer — Solo Attorney Admin & Drafting Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not legal work product or advice

Every output is an **internal first draft** for licensed-attorney review. This
kit is a productivity aid for administrative and drafting tasks. It is **not**
legal advice, not legal work product, and not a substitute for a licensed
attorney's professional judgment. Nothing it produces should reach a client,
opposing party, or court without attorney review.

## Unauthorized practice of law (UPL)

Providing legal advice or legal services without a license is unlawful, and
attorneys remain fully responsible for supervising any tool they use. Position and
use this kit strictly as **administrative and first-draft support**:

- No output constitutes legal advice.
- Every template carries an **"attorney review required"** gate.
- Non-attorneys (e.g. paralegals) must work under attorney supervision consistent
  with their jurisdiction's rules.

## AI hallucinates law — verify everything

Large language models fabricate plausible-looking case citations, statutes, and
quotations. Lawyers have been **sanctioned** for filing AI-invented cases. The
prompts in this kit deliberately avoid generating citations and insert
`[LEGAL BASIS — ATTORNEY TO INSERT]` placeholders. Verify all legal authority,
rules, and jurisdiction-specific requirements against primary sources yourself.

## Confidentiality & privilege

You owe clients a duty of confidentiality. Do not paste privileged or
confidential client information into public AI tools. Redact identifiers, use a
data-protected/enterprise model with appropriate safeguards, and obtain client
consent where your rules require it. Consider your jurisdiction's ethics opinions
on generative AI.

## No outcome guarantees; competence & jurisdiction

Do not promise or predict legal outcomes. Rules of professional conduct,
UPL definitions, and AI-use ethics guidance vary by jurisdiction. You are
responsible for compliance with the rules of every jurisdiction in which you
practice.
