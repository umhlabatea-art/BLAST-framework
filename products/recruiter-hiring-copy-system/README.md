# Recruiter Hiring-Copy System

**Fill your roles faster with EEOC-aware, copy-paste hiring prompts.**
A done-for-you system for recruiters and talent-acquisition teams: 12 prompts
covering the full req-to-offer workflow, a Notion hiring tracker, and a workflow
doc that bakes inclusive-language and skills-based defaults into every output.

- **Tier:** Core — **$49**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion tracker
  (`workflow.md`) + compliance disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** In-house recruiters, agency recruiters, TA leads, hiring managers
- **License:** Personal use (single recruiter). Team/commercial license available.

## Why this exists

67% of organizations now use AI in recruitment (enterprise leaders ~78%), yet
the *paid* market is thin — recruiters are drowning in free, generic prompt
lists with no packaged workflow and no compliance layer. This kit is the
system: prompts that already default to inclusive language and skills-based
criteria, wired into a tracker so nothing falls through.

## The ROI

Job-description creation time drops materially with AI, and the same compression
applies to screening questions, outreach, and rejection emails. Two hours saved
a week is ~100 hours a year redeployed to actual candidate conversations — the
part of the job software can't do. **This kit costs less than the time it saves
in the first week.**

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six tasks:
   - Job descriptions (inclusive-language default)
   - Boolean / screening search strings
   - Structured screening questions
   - Candidate outreach / InMail
   - Interview scorecards (skills-based rubric)
   - Rejection emails + offer-letter drafts
2. **`workflow.md`** — the req-to-offer pipeline, a Notion hiring-tracker
   schema, and the human checkpoints that keep you compliant.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — the EEOC / anti-discrimination overlay and draft-review
   boilerplate.

## How to use it

1. Open `prompts.md`, find the task you're on, copy the prompt.
2. Replace every `[BRACKET]` with your specifics.
3. Paste into your AI tool of choice (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).
4. **Review the output against `DISCLAIMER.md` before it reaches a candidate.**
5. Log the role in the Notion tracker from `workflow.md`.

## The compliance edge

Casual sellers won't touch HR prompts because of EEOC exposure. That's the moat:
every prompt here defaults to inclusive language, skills-based criteria, and
"no protected-class proxies." See `DISCLAIMER.md`. The seller of the prompt is
not your employment lawyer — outputs are drafts for your review.
