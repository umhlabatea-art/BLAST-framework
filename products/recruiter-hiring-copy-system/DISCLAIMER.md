# Disclaimer — Recruiter Hiring-Copy System

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not decisions

Every output produced by these prompts is a **draft for human review**. This kit
is a productivity aid, not employment-law advice, not a hiring-decision engine,
and not a substitute for your organization's counsel or HR policy. You — the
recruiter or employer — are responsible for every communication and decision,
regardless of the fact that AI helped draft it.

## EEOC / anti-discrimination overlay

Hiring is regulated. In the United States, the EEOC enforces anti-discrimination
law (Title VII, ADA, ADEA and related statutes), and AI-assisted hiring is under
active scrutiny for **adverse impact**. The prompts in this kit default to:

- **Skills-based criteria** — assess the ability to do the job, not proxies for
  it.
- **Inclusive language** — no gender-, age-, or ability-coded wording.
- **No protected-class proxies** — do not screen on or reference age, race, sex,
  disability, national origin, religion, pregnancy, or veteran status, or
  stand-ins for them (e.g. graduation year, "digital native", "recent grad").
- **Structured, consistent process** — the same questions and scorecards for
  every candidate for a given role.

Keep these defaults in place. If you weaken them, you take on the risk.

## Never let the model decide

Do not use these prompts (or any AI tool) to **rank, score-and-reject, or filter
out** candidates automatically. AI drafts; a human recruiter reviews and decides.
This human-in-the-loop step is the single most important control for limiting
disparate-impact exposure.

## Jurisdiction

Employment law varies by country, state, and locality (e.g. NYC Local Law 144 on
automated employment decision tools; the EU AI Act's treatment of hiring as
high-risk; South Africa's Employment Equity Act and POPIA for candidate data).
The end-user carries liability. Confirm the rules for your jurisdiction and get
qualified legal review of your process before relying on it.

## Data

Do not paste candidate personal data into a public AI tool in violation of your
privacy obligations (GDPR / POPIA / CCPA). Anonymize where you can; use an
enterprise/data-protected model where you can't.
