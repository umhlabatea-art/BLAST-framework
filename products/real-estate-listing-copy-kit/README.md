## Why this exists (and a positioning note)

Listing copy takes 30–60 minutes each and is compressible to under 5. Demand is
proven — there are live competitors from ~$9 to ~$100. But generic real estate is
a **saturated, price-collapsed** category. To command more than a rock-bottom
price, position around a **sub-niche** (e.g. luxury listings or property-
investor marketing) and around the one thing amateurs get wrong: **fair-housing
compliance**. This kit's prompts default to fair-housing-safe language, which is
the differentiator worth paying for.

## The ROI

An agent who writes 3–5 listings a week reclaims 2–4 hours weekly — time that
goes back into showings and client relationships, the commission-generating
work. Faster listings also mean faster time-to-market for sellers.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six tasks:
   - MLS listing descriptions (fair-housing-safe)
   - Buyer emails
   - Seller emails / listing presentations
   - Social captions
   - Market-update content
   - Negotiation & offer scripts
2. **`workflow.md`** — a listing-to-close content pipeline and a Notion listing
   tracker.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — the fair-housing (Fair Housing Act) overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the task, copy the prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]`.
2. **Describe the property, never the buyer.** Fair housing means marketing the
   home's features, not who "should" live there (see `DISCLAIMER.md`).
3. Review every output for fair-housing compliance before publishing.
4. Log the listing in the Notion tracker.

## The compliance edge

The seller of AI-written listing copy is responsible for fair-housing compliance
**regardless of AI origin**. This kit defaults to describing the property, avoids
protected-class steering language, and flags risky phrasing — the exact thing
casual prompt-packs ignore.
