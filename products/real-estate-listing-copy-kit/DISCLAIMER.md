## Fair-housing overlay

Real estate marketing is regulated by the federal Fair Housing Act (and state and
local equivalents). It is unlawful to make, print, or publish any statement that
indicates a **preference, limitation, or discrimination** based on race, color,
religion, sex, disability, familial status, or national origin — and many
jurisdictions add protected classes (age, source of income, sexual orientation,
and more).

Critically: **you are responsible for fair-housing compliance regardless of
whether a human or an AI wrote the copy.** The prompts in this kit default to:

- **Describe the property, not the people.** Market features, not who "should"
  live there.
- **No steering language** — nothing that directs buyers toward or away from an
  area based on a protected class (including proxies like "safe neighborhood,"
  "family-friendly," "walking distance to churches," "perfect for young
  professionals").
- **Factual amenities only** — schools, transit, and distances stated as facts,
  not demographic signals.

Run the fair-housing review prompt on anything you're unsure about, and cut
flagged phrasing.

## No invented data or guarantees

Do not publish statistics the model invented — use only verified data. Do not
promise a sale, a price, or a timeline you can't guarantee.

## Jurisdiction & data

Protected classes and advertising rules vary by state and city. Confirm the rules
for your market and follow your brokerage's compliance process. Don't paste
clients' confidential information into public AI tools.
