# Physician Practice-Admin Kit

**Non-clinical admin, done faster — so you get time back from paperwork.**
A done-for-you system for physicians and clinical administrators covering the
**administrative, non-clinical** writing around a practice: patient-reminder
templates, referral letters, practice-marketing content, and staff SOPs.
**Explicitly not for diagnosis, treatment, or any clinical decision-making.**

- **Tier:** Core — **$79**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion tracker
  (`workflow.md`) + disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** Physicians, practice managers, clinical administrators, front-office
  leads
- **License:** Personal use (single practice). Group/organization license
  available.

## Why this exists

Physicians spend roughly two hours on documentation and administrative work for
every hour of patient care — a leading driver of burnout. Ambient AI scribes have
shown large documentation-time savings at scale. This kit targets the **non-
clinical** slice of that burden: the reminder templates, referral letters,
marketing, and SOPs that don't require clinical judgment and don't touch
diagnosis.

## The ROI

Administrative time is time away from patients (or from rest). Systematizing the
repetitive, non-clinical writing gives that time back — without ever asking an AI
to make a clinical call.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six **non-clinical** tasks:
   - Patient appointment / recall reminder templates
   - Referral & coordination letters (administrative)
   - Practice-marketing content (services, hours, welcome)
   - New-patient onboarding & FAQ content
   - Staff SOPs & internal process docs
   - Patient-satisfaction / feedback requests
2. **`workflow.md`** — an admin-content pipeline and a Notion practice-ops
   tracker, with the "no PHI in public tools" rule front and center.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — the HIPAA + non-clinical-only overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the **administrative** task, copy the prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]` with
   **generic, non-patient-identifying** information.
2. **Never enter protected health information (PHI) into a public AI tool**, and
   never use these prompts for clinical decisions (see `DISCLAIMER.md`).
3. Review every patient-facing output for accuracy before use.
4. Track templates and SOPs in the Notion practice-ops tracker.

## The compliance edge (severe)

Medicine carries the heaviest liability overlay: HIPAA, medical liability, and
hallucination risk. This kit is positioned **strictly as non-clinical admin** —
it will not help with diagnosis, treatment, dosing, or clinical decision support,
and it keeps PHI out of the loop. That discipline is the product.
