# Disclaimer — Physician Practice-Admin Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Administrative, non-clinical use only

This kit is for **practice administration only** — reminders, administrative
letters, marketing, onboarding, SOPs, and feedback requests. It is **not** a
clinical tool. Do **not** use it for, and it must never be relied on for:

- diagnosis, differential diagnosis, or triage,
- treatment, medication, or dosing decisions,
- interpretation of results, or
- any clinical decision support.

Clinical judgment belongs to a licensed clinician using clinically-validated
tools. Outputs are drafts for human review, not medical advice, and create no
provider-patient relationship.

## HIPAA & PHI

Do **not** enter protected health information (PHI) — patient names, dates,
medical record numbers, or any identifying detail — into a public AI tool. Public
models are not HIPAA-compliant and typically have no Business Associate Agreement
(BAA) in place. The prompts default to **merge fields and generic placeholders**;
real patient data is merged only inside your HIPAA-compliant EHR / practice-
management / secure-messaging systems. Where a vendor will process PHI, ensure a
BAA and appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Hallucination & accuracy

AI can produce confident, incorrect content. Review every patient-facing output
for accuracy before use. Never let generated text stand in for verified clinical
or billing information.

## No medical claims or guarantees

Marketing content must not make unsupported medical claims, guarantee outcomes,
or imply results. Follow applicable advertising rules and your board's guidance on
professional advertising.

## Jurisdiction & liability

Requirements vary (HIPAA and state privacy laws in the US; GDPR/POPIA and local
health-data rules elsewhere; medical-board advertising rules). Medical liability
sits with the practice and its clinicians. Confirm what applies to you and obtain
qualified legal/compliance review before relying on this kit.
