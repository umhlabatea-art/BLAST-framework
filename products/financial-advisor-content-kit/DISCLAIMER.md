# Disclaimer — Financial Advisor Content Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not advice

Every output produced by these prompts is a **draft for supervisory review**.
This kit is a productivity aid for content creation. It is **not** investment,
legal, or tax advice; it is not a compliance program; and it does not replace
your firm's CCO, principal, or counsel. You and your firm are responsible for
every communication that is sent, regardless of the fact that AI helped draft it.

## FINRA Rule 2210

FINRA Rule 2210 (Communications with the Public) applies to AI-generated
communications **identically** to human-written ones. That means:

- **Pre-use principal approval** for retail communications (with limited
  exceptions), *before* first use.
- **Fair and balanced** content — benefits paired with risks; nothing false,
  exaggerated, misleading, or promissory.
- **No performance guarantees** and no projections presented as certainties.
- **Recordkeeping** — retain communications and approvals per the books-and-
  records rules.

The prompts in this kit default to disclosure blocks and fair-balance language,
and the workflow in `workflow.md` builds in the pre-use approval and archiving
steps. Keep them in place.

## SEC Marketing Rule 206(4)-1

For SEC-registered advisers, the Marketing Rule governs advertisements,
including testimonials and endorsements (which carry specific disclosure and
oversight conditions) and any performance presentation. Do not publish
testimonials, endorsements, or performance figures produced by these prompts
without confirming they meet the rule's requirements.

## Do not overstate AI ("AI-washing")

Do not claim or imply the firm uses AI to select investments, manage portfolios,
or generate returns unless that is verifiably true. The SEC's 2024 settlements
against advisers for misrepresenting their AI use are the cautionary precedent.
Describe only what the tool actually does.

## Human-in-the-loop

A licensed human reviews and approves every output before it is sent, and no
individualized investment advice is delivered by the model itself. This is the
regulator-expected baseline for GenAI in this context.

## Data & jurisdiction

Never paste non-public client personal or financial information into a public AI
tool. Requirements vary by regulator and jurisdiction (FINRA, SEC, state
regulators, and outside the US, local equivalents plus data laws such as POPIA).
Confirm the rules that apply to you and obtain qualified compliance and legal
review before relying on this kit.
