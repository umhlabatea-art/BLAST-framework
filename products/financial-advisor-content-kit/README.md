with a human checkpoint.

## Why this exists

An entire vendor category (compliance-aware AI content tools) exists because
advisors clearly pay to solve this — but those are expensive subscriptions. This
is the lightweight wedge below them: a one-time kit of pre-approved templates and
required disclosures that produces content already shaped for supervisory review.

Generic "finance" prompt bundles have **zero** FINRA/SEC awareness, which is
dangerous for a regulated advisor. This kit's entire reason to exist is that it
does.

## The ROI

Advisor time is scarce and expensive, and client-facing hours are the
constraint. A repeatable content engine — market commentary, review summaries,
prospecting, newsletters — reclaims hours without putting the firm's compliance
posture at risk. The disclaimer/workflow layer removes buyer risk, which is
exactly why this tier is priced above generic packs.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six tasks:
   - Client review / meeting summaries
   - Market-commentary emails (fair-balance default)
   - Prospecting sequences
   - Meeting-note structuring
   - Newsletter content
   - Seminar / webinar invitations
   Each prompt includes a **disclosure block** and a **fair-balance** default.
2. **`workflow.md`** — the create → review → archive pipeline, a Notion
   compliance-review tracker, and the human supervisory checkpoint.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — FINRA Rule 2210 + SEC Marketing Rule 206(4)-1 overlay.

## How to use it

1. Draft with the relevant prompt; keep the disclosure block and fair-balance
   language.
2. **Route the draft to your CCO / principal for pre-use approval** before it
   reaches any client or prospect (this is the whole point — see `workflow.md`).
3. Archive the approved communication per your recordkeeping obligations.
4. Log it in the Notion review tracker.

## The compliance edge

FINRA Rule 2210 governs AI-generated communications **identically** to
human-written ones — pre-use approval, fair balance, recordkeeping. The SEC
Marketing Rule 206(4)-1 applies to advertisements. The 2024 SEC "AI-washing"
settlements are the cautionary tale for overstating AI use. This kit is built
around *pre-approved templates + required disclosures + a human-checkpoint
workflow* — precisely what the regulatory literature prescribes. See
`DISCLAIMER.md`.
