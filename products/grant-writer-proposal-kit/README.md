# Grant Writer Proposal Kit

**Draft the whole proposal faster — from LOI to impact report.**
A done-for-you system for grant writers and nonprofit development staff: 12
prompts covering the grant lifecycle — letters of inquiry, needs statements,
budget narratives, funder research summaries, and impact reports — with an
accuracy-to-funders layer built in.

- **Tier:** Core — **$47**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion tracker
  (`workflow.md`) + disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** Freelance grant writers, nonprofit development staff, program
  directors
- **License:** Personal use (single writer). Organization/team license available.

## Why this exists

A single proposal can take 20–60 hours, and grant writers bill $50–$150/hr. The
category is **low saturation** and the ROI is enormous — cited examples show
$250K awards for ~10 hours of work. Compressing the drafting of each proposal
component is high-leverage: more submissions, same hours, more funding won.

## The ROI

If the kit saves even 5–10 hours per proposal, a writer either bills more
proposals or delivers each faster. For an in-house team, faster drafting means
more grants pursued per cycle — and grant ROI dwarfs the tool cost.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six components:
   - Letters of inquiry (LOIs)
   - Needs / problem statements
   - Program design & goals/objectives
   - Budget narratives
   - Funder research summaries
   - Impact / final reports
2. **`workflow.md`** — a prospect → submit → report pipeline and a Notion grants
   calendar.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — accuracy-to-funders / no-fabricated-data overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the component, copy the prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]` with your
   organization's real information.
2. **Feed it real data — never let the model invent statistics, outcomes, or
   citations** (see `DISCLAIMER.md`).
3. Edit into the funder's required format and your organization's voice.
4. Track deadlines and reporting obligations in the Notion grants calendar.

## The edge

Bound to the actual grant lifecycle, with a hard rule against fabricated data —
because a made-up statistic in a funder proposal isn't just weak writing, it's a
credibility and compliance risk that can cost the relationship.
