# Disclaimer — Grant Writer Proposal Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not submissions

Every output is a draft for your review and editing. This kit is a productivity
aid; it does not replace your professional judgment, your organization's review
process, or a funder's specific requirements.

## Accuracy to funders is non-negotiable

Grant proposals and reports are representations to funders, often backed by a
signed agreement. **Never let the model invent or estimate:**

- statistics, prevalence figures, or "research shows" claims,
- program outcomes or numbers served,
- citations or sources,
- funder facts (priorities, grant sizes, eligibility).

The prompts default to using only data you provide and to flagging unverified
claims with `[VERIFY]` / `[NEEDS SOURCE]`. Resolve every flag before submission.
A fabricated figure can cost the funder relationship and, in a report, may breach
your grant agreement or constitute misrepresentation.

## Protect beneficiary privacy

Use anonymized stories and obtain consent before sharing beneficiary details. Do
not paste identifiable personal data of the people you serve into a public AI
tool. Follow applicable privacy law (HIPAA if health-related, FERPA if education,
GDPR/POPIA/CCPA) and your organization's data policy.

## Honesty in reporting

Report real results, including objectives not fully met. Funders value candor;
overstating impact is both an ethical and a compliance problem.

## Not legal, financial, or tax advice

Nothing here is legal, financial, or tax advice (including on nonprofit
compliance or grant-agreement terms). Consult qualified professionals where
needed.
