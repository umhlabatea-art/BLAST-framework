# Disclaimer — SaaS Customer Success Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not commitments

Every output is a draft for your review. This kit is a productivity aid for
customer-facing content. It does not replace your judgment as a CS professional
or your company's approved messaging, legal, and security guidelines.

## Don't overpromise

Customer-facing content can create expectations — and, in some cases, contractual
or legal exposure. Do **not** promise:

- **SLAs, uptime, or support-response guarantees** not in the customer's contract,
- **roadmap items or features** that don't exist or aren't committed,
- **outcomes or results** you can't stand behind.

The prompts default to using only real capabilities and committed items. Keep
that discipline — an overpromise from a "helpful" nudge is still a promise your
company has to keep.

## Use only real data

QBRs, reports, and success stories must use verified usage and outcome data.
Never present a metric the model generated or estimated as fact.

## Protect customer data

Do not paste customer PII, usage data tied to identifiable people, or
confidential account information into a public AI tool. Follow your company's data
handling and security policies and use approved/enterprise tooling. Data
obligations (GDPR, POPIA, CCPA, plus your DPAs) sit with you and your company.

## Brand & voice

Outputs must be adapted to your product's approved brand voice and terminology
before they reach a customer.
