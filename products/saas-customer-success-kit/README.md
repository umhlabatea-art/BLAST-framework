# SaaS Customer Success Kit

**Cut time-to-value and save at-risk accounts — with a repeatable content engine.**
A done-for-you system for customer success and onboarding teams: 12 prompts for
the lifecycle touchpoints that drive activation, retention, and expansion — from
onboarding sequences to QBR decks to churn-save scripts.

- **Tier:** Core — **$69**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion tracker
  (`workflow.md`) + disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** CSMs, onboarding specialists, CS leaders, founder-led CS
- **License:** Personal use (single CSM). Team/company license available.

## Why this exists

Time-to-value is a board-level metric, and CS teams live on a company card with
SaaS budgets — premium tolerance is high. Yet the daily content (onboarding
emails, in-app nudges, QBR narratives, churn-saves, help docs) is written from
scratch over and over. This kit turns it into a repeatable, on-brand engine.

## The ROI

Faster activation and better retention move revenue directly. A CSM who reclaims
two hours a week on content spends it on the high-touch conversations that
actually save and grow accounts — the part of the role that moves NRR.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six touchpoints:
   - Onboarding email sequences
   - In-app / lifecycle messaging
   - QBR / business-review narratives
   - Churn-save & win-back scripts
   - Help-doc / knowledge-base drafts
   - Expansion / upsell outreach
2. **`workflow.md`** — a customer-lifecycle pipeline and a Notion account-health
   tracker.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — brand-voice, no-overpromising-SLAs, data overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the touchpoint, copy the prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]` with account
   context.
2. Match it to your product's brand voice and factual capabilities.
3. **Never promise a capability, SLA, or roadmap item that isn't real** (see
   `DISCLAIMER.md`).
4. Track account health and touchpoints in the Notion tracker.

## The edge

Bound to the actual CS lifecycle and its metrics (activation, health, NRR), with
guardrails that keep customer-facing promises accurate — the difference between
a helpful nudge and a commitment your product can't keep.
