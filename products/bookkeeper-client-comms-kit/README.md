# Bookkeeper & Accountant Client-Comms Kit

**Stop rewriting the same client emails — systematize them.**
A done-for-you system for bookkeepers and accountants: 12 prompts for the client
communications you send every month, from onboarding to month-end summaries to
the polite-but-firm chase for missing records.

- **Tier:** Core — **$37**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion tracker
  (`workflow.md`) + disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** Independent bookkeepers, small accounting firms, CPAs
- **License:** Personal use (single practitioner). Firm/team license available.

## Why this exists

Bookkeeping clerks are an automating, shrinking base — but the ones who remain
are AI-hungry and moving up the value chain into advisory. The repetitive client
communications (onboarding, engagement letters, month-end recaps, reminder
sequences) are pure overhead. Systematizing them frees time for the billable
advisory work that survives automation.

## The ROI

Bookkeepers bill $25–$75/hr, CPAs $150–$400/hr. Two hours a week of communication
overhead reclaimed is $2,500–$40,000/yr of capacity depending on your rate — and
it goes toward the higher-value advisory conversations clients actually pay a
premium for.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six tasks:
   - Client onboarding emails
   - Engagement-letter drafts
   - Month-end / period summaries
   - Chase / reminder sequences (missing records)
   - Advisory memos (plain-English)
   - Price-increase & scope-change notices
2. **`workflow.md`** — a client-lifecycle pipeline and a Notion client tracker.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — "not tax/accounting advice" + engagement-letter overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the communication, copy the prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]`.
2. **Never paste client financial data or PII into a public AI tool** — describe
   generically or use a data-protected model (see `DISCLAIMER.md`).
3. Review for accuracy — the numbers are yours, the model just drafts the words.
4. Track the client and cadence in the Notion tracker.

## The edge

Built for the actual monthly rhythm of a bookkeeping practice, with engagement-
letter and advisory-memo drafts that stay firmly on the "we prepared; you should
have it reviewed" side of the line — never crossing into unlicensed tax advice.
