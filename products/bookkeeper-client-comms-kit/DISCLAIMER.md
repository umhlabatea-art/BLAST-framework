# Disclaimer — Bookkeeper & Accountant Client-Comms Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not advice

Every output is a draft for your review. This kit is a productivity aid for
client communication. It is **not** tax, accounting, audit, or legal advice, and
it does not replace your professional judgment or your client's need for a
qualified CPA, tax preparer, or attorney where those services are required.

## Stay within scope of practice

Bookkeeping communications summarize and organize the books. They do **not**:

- state tax positions or give tax-planning advice,
- render audit or assurance opinions, or
- provide legal advice.

The prompts default to framing recommendations as "considerations" and to
routing tax/legal questions to the appropriate licensed professional. Keep those
guardrails — offering unlicensed tax or legal advice creates liability and may
violate professional rules.

## Client data protection

Do **not** paste client financial data, account numbers, Social Security/Tax IDs,
or other PII into a public AI tool. This can breach confidentiality obligations
and data-protection law (GDPR, POPIA, CCPA, and financial-privacy rules like the
GLBA in the US). Describe situations generically, or use a data-protected /
enterprise model with appropriate safeguards.

## Verify every figure

The model drafts wording; it must never originate or calculate a financial
figure. Provide the numbers yourself and confirm them before any summary is sent.

## Engagement letters

The engagement-letter prompt produces a **starting template only**. Have it
reviewed by qualified counsel and adapted to your jurisdiction and firm before
use.
