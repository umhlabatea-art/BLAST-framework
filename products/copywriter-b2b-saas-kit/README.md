## Why this exists (and why it's sub-niched)

Generic marketing/copy is the **most saturated** category on every marketplace —
prices have collapsed there. The only way to command a real price is to escape
the mega-pack war with a **sub-niche**. This kit picks the most lucrative one:
**B2B SaaS lifecycle copy**, where conversion copywriters bill $100–$200/hr and a
single email sequence can be worth $2,000+.

## The ROI

At $0.70/word average and $2,000+ per email sequence, a freelancer who cuts
first-draft time in half doubles throughput on the assets that actually pay. Two
hours saved a week is ~100 hours a year of billable capacity reclaimed.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six asset types:
   - Lifecycle / onboarding email sequences
   - Feature-launch & announcement emails
   - Landing-page copy (hero → CTA)
   - Ad copy variations
   - SEO content briefs
   - Social calendars
2. **`workflow.md`** — a research → draft → client-review pipeline, a Notion
   content calendar, and the swipe-file habit.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — FTC truth-in-advertising / substantiation overlay.

## How to use it

1. Pick the asset, copy its prompt, fill every `[BRACKET]` with client specifics.
2. Feed it your research inputs (positioning, ICP, voice-of-customer quotes).
3. Edit for the client's brand voice — the draft is a starting point, your craft
   is the product.
4. Verify every claim against the client's substantiation (see `DISCLAIMER.md`).
5. Track it in the Notion content calendar.

## The edge

Sub-niched to the highest-paying corner of copy, and every prompt defaults to
**claim-substantiation discipline** — you never ship a performance claim a SaaS
client can't back up, which protects both of you from FTC exposure.
