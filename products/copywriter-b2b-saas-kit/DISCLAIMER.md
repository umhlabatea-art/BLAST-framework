# Disclaimer — B2B SaaS Copywriter Kit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not final copy

Every output is a first draft. Your craft, judgment, and the client's brand voice
are the actual deliverable. Review and rewrite before anything ships.

## Truth-in-advertising (FTC) overlay

Marketing copy is regulated. In the US, the FTC requires that advertising claims
be **truthful, not misleading, and substantiated** *before* they run — and both
the advertiser and, potentially, those who create the claims can bear
responsibility. The prompts in this kit default to claim-substantiation
discipline and tag claims `[PROVABLE]` / `[NEEDS PROOF]`. Keep that discipline:

- **No unsubstantiated performance or results claims.** "Cut costs 40%" needs
  the client's data behind it.
- **Comparative claims** ("better than X") must be substantiated and not
  misleading.
- **Testimonials and endorsements** must reflect genuine experience and follow
  FTC guidance (including disclosure of material connections).
- **No fake urgency or deceptive design** ("dark patterns").

The client is the advertiser and owns final responsibility for claims, but do not
knowingly draft a claim they can't back up.

## No plagiarism, respect IP

Do not pass off competitor copy or others' content as original. Verify the model
hasn't reproduced protected material.

## Jurisdiction & data

Advertising and privacy rules vary (FTC in the US; ASA/CAP in the UK; consumer-
protection and POPIA/GDPR elsewhere). Don't paste a client's confidential
material into a public AI tool in violation of your agreement. Confirm what
applies to each client.
