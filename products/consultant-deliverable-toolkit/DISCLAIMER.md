# Disclaimer — Consultant Deliverable Toolkit

**Read this before using any prompt in this kit.**

## Drafts, not deliverables

Every output produced by these prompts is a **draft**. This kit is a productivity
aid; it does not replace your professional judgment, analysis, or accountability
to the client. Your expertise is the deliverable — the prompts just clear the
blank page. Review and edit every output before it reaches a client or the
public.

## Verify every claim

AI models can produce plausible but wrong figures, benchmarks, and citations. The
prompts mark numbers and claims with `[VERIFY]` and `[SOURCE?]` — do not remove
those markers until you have personally confirmed the underlying fact. Never
present an unverified model output as your analysis.

## Client confidentiality

Do not paste a client's confidential, proprietary, or non-public information into
a public AI tool. Doing so may breach your engagement terms, an NDA, or data-
protection law. Anonymize inputs (use an industry descriptor instead of the
client name, ranges instead of exact figures), or use a data-protected /
enterprise model where required. Protecting client information is your
responsibility.

## No professional-advice substitution

If your engagement touches regulated domains (legal, financial, tax, medical,
safety), these general-purpose prompts do not make you qualified in those
domains, and their outputs are not legal, financial, tax, or other professional
advice. Bring in the appropriate licensed professional where the work requires
it.

## Jurisdiction & data

Confidentiality and data obligations vary by contract and jurisdiction (GDPR,
POPIA, CCPA, and client-specific terms). You carry the liability for how client
data is handled. Confirm what applies to your engagements.
