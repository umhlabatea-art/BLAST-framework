# Consultant Deliverable Toolkit

**Turn non-billable admin into billable hours.**
A done-for-you system for independent management consultants: 12 prompts bound to
the deliverables you actually produce — proposals, discovery frameworks,
executive summaries, slide narratives, client updates, case studies — plus a
Notion proposal pipeline to track it all.

- **Tier:** Core — **$59**
- **Format:** Prompt pack (`prompts.md`) + workflow doc & Notion pipeline
  (`workflow.md`) + disclaimer (`DISCLAIMER.md`)
- **Buyer:** Independent / boutique management consultants, fractional execs,
  strategy freelancers
- **License:** Personal use (single consultant). Team license available.

## Why this exists

Generalist "run your whole business" prompt packs aren't bound to consulting
*deliverables* — they're broad and shallow. Consultants lose 30–60 minutes of
non-billable admin per billable hour. This toolkit compresses that admin against
the specific artifacts a consulting engagement produces, so the saved time flows
back to billable work.

## The ROI

Independent consultants bill roughly $100–$350/hr. Two hours saved a week is
~100 hours a year — $10,000–$35,000 of recoverable capacity at those rates.
Because consultants treat tools as a tax-deductible business expense, the price
clears easily against a single reclaimed billable hour.

## What's inside (Core tier)

1. **`prompts.md`** — 12 prompts across six deliverables:
   - Proposals / SOWs
   - Discovery-call frameworks
   - Executive summaries
   - Slide narratives
   - Client update emails
   - Case-study write-ups
2. **`workflow.md`** — the lead → proposal → delivery pipeline and a Notion
   proposal-pipeline schema.
3. **`DISCLAIMER.md`** — draft-review discipline + client-confidentiality
   guardrails.

## How to use it

1. Pick the deliverable you're producing; copy its prompt.
2. Replace every `[BRACKET]` — the more specific your inputs, the better the
   draft.
3. **Review and edit before it reaches a client.** These are drafts; your
   judgment and expertise are the product.
4. Track the engagement in the Notion pipeline from `workflow.md`.

## The edge

Deliverable-bound, not generic. Every prompt is shaped to the artifact a client
actually pays for, and the workflow keeps your pipeline visible so proposals
don't stall. See `DISCLAIMER.md` for the confidentiality guardrails that protect
you and your clients.
